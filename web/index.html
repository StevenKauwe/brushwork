Light
Drag on the canvas to lay down a shape; shapes paint back to front. Subjects are modelled in the light and get smaller brushes and thicker paint. A shadow shape says where a shadow falls: the painter glazes it over what is there, in a colour mixed from it and the light. A glow is a light: luminous paint that lights the sky and the paint round it, and the scene's light comes from it. The painting runs on your device, in a Web Worker: on its graphics card where the browser offers WebGPU, otherwise on its processor (the label on the canvas says which). Source on GitHub (MIT).
